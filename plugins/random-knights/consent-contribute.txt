Share your AI use numbers with the community totals?

This is separate from connecting your account. It is OFF unless you say yes.

What we send, one small summary per day:
  - the day (no time of day), the model name, the tool name (for example
    claude-code), and whether the token counts were reported or estimated
  - how many tokens went in and out and how many responses there were
We work out the energy, carbon and water ourselves from those token counts.

What we never send:
  your prompts, replies, file or folder names, project names, API keys,
  your computer's name, your location, or the exact time of any response.

Who can see it:
  Nobody sees your numbers alone. For up to 8 days we hold them and can match
  them to your account, only to stop double counting and to let you delete
  them. Then they are added into monthly totals. A total is shown only when
  at least 50 people are in it, and counts are rounded. The totals are public
  on the CE report and in the rand0m.ai app.

How to stop and delete:
  aieds contribute off      stop sending now
  aieds contribute delete   remove numbers not yet added into a total
Numbers already added into a monthly total cannot be taken out, because the
total no longer holds anything that points to you.

You must be 13 or older. You can say no and keep using everything else.
